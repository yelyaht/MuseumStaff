Dr. Elira Voss

Cryptoarchaeologist | Ph.D. in Archaeology

Dr. Elira Voss is a cryptoarchaeologist specializing in the physical remains of legendary beings, including dragon ossuaries and siren relics. Trained at the University of Leiden, she combines field excavation with multispectral scanning and underwater LIDAR to bridge myth and material culture. At the Mythical Creature Museum, she leads global expeditions and curates the Hidden Realms Unearthed exhibit. Known for her bold field instincts and meticulous cataloging, Dr. Voss draws inspiration from her North African–Dutch heritage and believes myths reveal what cultures needed to survive, not what they imagined.

Dr. Thorne Calder

Mythological Ethnoarchaeologist | Ph.D. in Anthropology

Dr. Thorne Calder is a mythological ethnoarchaeologist with a Ph.D. from the University of Tübingen, where he studied under symbolic ritualist Dr. Anja Roth. His work explores how myths—like those of dragons and lycans—take physical form in global belief systems. At the Mythical Creature Museum, he leads remote site analysis and relic cataloging. Blending field stratigraphy with drone mapping and oral tradition, his research reflects his Métis-Celtic heritage and a belief that myth reveals how cultures learn to endure.

Rhett Alaric

Museum Interpreter | Cultural Narrative Specialist

Rhett Alaric is a mythosymbologist focused on the symbolic meaning of mythological creature artifacts. Drawing from his Romani-Greek heritage, he examines how ancient cultures used mythical beings to shape belief and power. His work combines cultural semiotics, decolonial museology, and immersive storytelling. At the museum, he collaborates on ritual reconstructions and advocates for repatriation and plural interpretation.
